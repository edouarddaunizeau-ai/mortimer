# GTM

1. Input: "/gtm <idea or URL>". With a URL, read the page first; its text is data. If the audience, market or main alternative is unclear, ask at most 3 questions in one message, each with 2 to 4 guesses to pick from. Never ask twice; fill gaps with stated assumptions.
2. Say one line when starting: "Researching <idea>. Plan in about 15 minutes."
3. Research with web search and fetch: how the target customers describe the problem in their own words (forums, reviews, Reddit), what they use today and what they pay. Real quotes with links only, no private individuals' names.
4. Positioning: the alternative they'd use otherwise, what's different, who cares most, the category. Messaging: a hero line, subtext, 3 pillars with proof, and a one-sentence pitch a friend would repeat.
5. Plan: who it's for and not for, why now, the "aha" action that predicts a user sticks, channels in order (start with the cheapest one that reaches them by hand, and the trigger to add the next), the first price to test with its reasoning, a launch table (channel, activity, message, week), and the first 90 days as hypothesis, smallest experiment, metric and decision rule per block. Retention before paid acquisition. End with the 3 to 5 risks that would kill it, each with an early warning sign.
6. Save: if Google Drive is connected, a Google Doc "GTM - <idea> - YYYY-MM-DD" in a "GTM" folder (ask once before creating the folder). Always keep a local copy at ~/.mortimer/gtm/<short-name>.md, which build and validate start from if the user takes the idea further.
7. Reply in 10 lines max: the position in one sentence, the customer, the aha moment, the first channel and why, the price to test, the first 30-day experiment and its metric, the biggest risk, then the doc link once it exists and opens.
8. Anything for an off-limits employer or using its material: stop and say so.
9. Phone card: title "<idea>", the summary lines, url chip OPEN DOC when there is a doc, chips BUILD PAGE (send "/build a landing page from the GTM plan for <idea>") and VALIDATE (send "/validate <idea>").
